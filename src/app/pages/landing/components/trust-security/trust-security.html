Verification process

Identity checks, secure document handling, and account privacy controls help confirm who is participating. Business applicants may complete additional verification where applicable.

  • Secure handling of submitted documents
  • Access limited to authorized account sessions

Risk disclosure

Peer-to-peer investing and borrowing involve risk. Investors may lose some or all capital. Borrowers remain responsible for obligations under accepted terms. Aurexion does not guarantee returns, repayment schedules, or capital protection.
